How we work

Four steps, in this order.

  1. 01

    You tell us what you need

    A resume, a project, a presentation, an assignment or a subject you are finding hard. Say it in your own words.

  2. 02

    We agree a plan with you

    What will be done, in what order, in plain language.

  3. 03

    We work it through together

    You stay in the picture. Questions are welcome at any point.

  4. 04

    You review, we refine

    We adjust until the work feels right to you.
